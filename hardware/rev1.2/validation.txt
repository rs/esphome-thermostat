THERMOSTAT BASE PCB — REVISION 1.2

Changes
- Retain the revision 1.1 mirrored H1 header for component-side face-to-face mating.
- Add U4 LM66100DCKR (LCSC C2869734) between buck +5V and +5V_DISPLAY.
  VIN pin 1 is on buck +5V; VOUT pin 6 and CE pin 3 are on +5V_DISPLAY.
  GND pin 2 and unused ST pin 5 go to GND; NC pin 4 is unconnected.
  H1 pin 2 is on +5V_DISPLAY. Buck feedback, bulk output capacitors, and TP2
  remain on the upstream +5V rail.
- Add C12 220 nF / 100 V X7R, CC0805KKX7R0BB224 (LCSC C513710),
  directly to U2 VIN pin 2 and PGND pin 1. Move C2 to provide room and
  route its supply through two vias to preserve clearance.
- Add C13/C14 1 uF local input/output decoupling for U4, using the existing
  CL10A105KB8NNNC component type.
- Move LED1's designator inside the board and enlarge the H1 mating label.

USB usage
Use the Waveshare USB-UART connector for programming with the base powered.
The Waveshare native USB connector is directly tied to its common VBUS;
the base-side LM66100 does not protect a host attached to that native port
from base power. USB-UART has a separate reverse-blocking circuit on the
Waveshare board. All grounds remain common.

Hardware validation after assembly
1. With all power removed, inspect H1 alignment and check for rail-to-ground
   shorts. Verify H1 pin 2 connects to U4 VOUT, not directly to buck +5V.
2. Apply 24 V DC only. Check TP1 (~24 V), TP2 (~5 V), H1 pin 2 (near 5 V,
   with the load-dependent U4 drop), and TP3 (~3.3 V) with the display fitted.
3. Apply USB-UART power only, with 24 V disconnected. Verify the display
   starts and measure reverse current into the buck side of U4.
4. Exercise both sources and each removal order. Check startup, full
   backlight, Wi-Fi activity, and speaker load; measure ripple and temperature.
   The LMR36510 remains a 1 A converter. Full-display peak-load margin has
   not been established by these design checks.
